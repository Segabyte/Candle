Income documentsManual for now

A T4, a payslip, a statement — the plan is to read the figures off them for you. That needs a private, secure way to handle the document, which this preview does not have yet, so nothing is uploaded anywhere and no file leaves your device. Until then, enter the figures yourself and they behave exactly the same.
