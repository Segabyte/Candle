    What this preview is. Five ways into the same date of birth: the long view (summers, chapters), Today (an editable log of all 24 hours), The pad (a tear-off day sheet), Tear-off (a themed line for every date) and the Scratchpad, which reads plain sentences and files them into the right day and hour.

    Next to build. An account, so the record follows you between devices; money — the target number, the trajectory, the gap; and a way to look back at the days you have already torn off.
